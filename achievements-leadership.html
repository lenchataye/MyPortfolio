1st Place

STEMpower Project

2023 STEM Innovation

I served my community by developing a multi-functional assistive technology project called Blind-Tech, built after directly conducting a survey and research with five visually impaired individuals to understand their real daily challenges. Based on their prioritized needs—safe navigation, object recognition, emergency communication, smart home control, and location tracking—I designed and prototyped integrated wearable and smart systems using sensors, microcontrollers, and mobile technology to provide practical and affordable solutions in one complete package. The project combined scientific research, engineering design, and community-centered problem solving, and it later won first place at both STEM and national science and engineering competitions.

2022-2023 Educational Leadership

First AID & Anti HIV/AIDS Director

2021-2023 Health Leadership

As the Director of the First Aid and Anti HIV/AIDS Club, I led a strong movement in my school and community to protect and educate young people about life saving skills and serious health risks. Under my leadership, we trained more than 400 youths on practical first aid skills and awareness about HIV/AIDS and other sexually transmitted diseases that are affecting many young people in our country. I worked closely with university clubs, invited health professionals to our school, and collaborated with organizations like the Ethiopian Red Cross Society and local hospitals to deliver real and practical training sessions. We organized programs in engaging ways including demonstrations, dramas, and open discussions so that students would truly understand and remember the message. This work created real awareness in both the school and the wider community, and it strengthened my leadership as someone who takes responsibility and acts when lives are at risk.

National Science Fair Champion

2022 Scientific Excellence

Winning first place at the National Science and Engineering Fair was one of the proudest moments of my life. I represented STEMpower with my project, Blind-Tech, a system I built after directly working with five visually impaired individuals and carefully studying their real daily challenges. Instead of guessing their problems, I sat with them, listened to them, and designed the solution based on their priorities — safe navigation, emergency support, smart home control, and object recognition — all combined into one affordable system. Competing on a national stage against top young innovators from across the country, I was honored to win the Gold Medal and be recognized as the National Science Fair Champion of 2024. This achievement was not only about winning a competition, but about proving that solutions built from real community needs can stand at the highest level in the country.

Academic Excellence

2025 Higher Education

My academic journey has been built on discipline, determination, and a strong love for learning. In Grade 8, I earned a scholarship to attend ODA Special Boarding School after achieving the highest score on the regional examination. I completed my high school education there, graduating every grade with straight A+ results in all courses and maintaining a perfect 4.00 GPA throughout my entire high school journey, including my overall cumulative GPA. After finishing my studies at ODA, I returned to my hometown in West Arsi Zone and took the national high school leaving examination at my local school, where I still managed to achieve one of the highest results. Many parents in my community see me as a role model for their children and often encourage them to follow my example, and during school breaks some even ask me to teach their children, which has allowed me to motivate many young students in my hometown to take education seriously and aim higher.
