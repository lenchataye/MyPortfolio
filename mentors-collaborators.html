Dr. Birhanu Bulcha

NASA Goddard Space Flight Center

Dr. Birhanu Bulcha

Research Mentor NASA 2022-Present

Research Collaboration Journey

Dr. Birhanu Bulcha is a Research Engineer and Principal Investigator at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center in the United States. After I presented my Blind Tech project at the national science and engineering competition, he noticed my work and began mentoring me. Dr Birhanu guided me in improving the design of my project and encouraged me to continue developing affordable technology for visually impaired people in Ethiopia. His mentorship helped me think about how engineering solutions can be made accessible for communities that do not have access to expensive technology.

Space Technology Research
Remote Sensing Applications
Earth Observation Systems

Dr. Birhanu Bulcha believed in my Blind Tech project and helped me improve it further. His guidance gave me the confidence to continue building technology that can truly help my community.

Professor Jelani Nelson

Harvard & UC Berkeley

Professor Jelani Nelson

Academic Mentor Harvard/UC Berkeley 2021-Present

Algorithmic Excellence Journey

Jelani Nelson is a Professor of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences at University of California, Berkeley, where he became Chair of the Computer Science Division in 2024 and Department Chair in 2025. Before joining UC Berkeley, he served as an Associate Professor at Harvard University. He is also a Research Scientist at Google, applying algorithm research to real world systems used by billions of people. I learned from Professor Jelani during the AddisCoder program at Addis Ababa University, where he taught advanced algorithms with great clarity and patience. His lectures helped me understand complex algorithm ideas while I was still in high school. He also supported my academic journey by covering my SAT examination fee and encouraging me to continue learning computer science at a deeper level.

Advanced Algorithms
Data Structures
Computational Theory

Professor Jelani Nelson helped me understand algorithms at a very young age and showed me that Ethiopian students can compete in the best universities in the world. His support and encouragement changed the way I see my future in computer science.

Adama Science and Technology University

Leading STEM Institution

Adama Science and Technology University

Academic Partner ASTU 2020-Present

Educational Innovation Partnership

Adama Science and Technology University (ASTU) is one of the leading science and technology universities in Ethiopia. The university supported my work by allowing me to use its ICT facilities and learning environment to teach programming and algorithms to talented Ethiopian students through the EAGLOPEN AlgoLab program. With their support, students from different parts of the country were able to access computers, internet, and structured training that many of them could not find in their local schools.

Curriculum Development
Lab Establishment
Student Training

Adama Science and Technology University gave me the environment and infrastructure to teach young Ethiopian students programming and algorithms. Their support helped expand the impact of my work through EAGLOPEN.

Engineer Fesseha Atlaw

Engineer Fesseha Atlaw

Ethiopic Pioneer UIUC 2022-Present

Machine Learning Excellence

Fesseha Atlaw is widely respected as the father of Ethiopic computing because of his pioneering work in digitizing the Ethiopian alphabet and developing early Ethiopic word processing systems. After learning about my Blind Tech project, he advised me to expand the system by including local languages and artificial intelligence. His advice helped me see how technology can better serve Ethiopian communities.
